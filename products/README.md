# Product photos

Upload photos here, named by product ID, for example `ie-craft-mm-apron.jpg`.
JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF all work. Square photos on a plain white background look best, ideally at least 1000×1000 pixels.

The full list of IDs is in `supplier/prices.csv`. Any product without a photo keeps its illustration.
Only use photos you own or that your supplier has given you permission to use.
